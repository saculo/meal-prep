# Glossary

## Unit
What an amount is measured in: g or kg (mass), ml or l (volume), or pieces. A reusable
ingredient has one or more units it can be used in. Amounts of one ingredient combine only
within one measure (g with kg, ml with l); mass, volume and pieces never combine.
_Avoid_: measure (for a single unit), pcs (in prose)

## Portions
How many servings a recipe's amounts make, or how many a planned meal is for. Optional on
both; missing counts as 1. A planned meal scales the recipe's amounts by its portions ÷ the
recipe's portions.
_Avoid_: servings, yield, batch
